Série trofeje, část SněhuláciTrophies series, part Snowmen

2024 · video · 2 min

This video is a by-product, a further part of the original snowman video, The Trophy Is Melting.

Here you can see 3D scans of snowmen built and scanned by different people from different countries with the Polycam app. There are surely a great many such snowmen, but this is just a selection of those shared publicly with the world, plus my own, which comes from my previous video The Trophy Is Melting. The image is accompanied by sound recorded from a 2011 Czech Television report on 3D scanning.

It has been a few years, but the principle is still the same. Today we can easily scan objects with our phones. And people point their phones at their rooms, their personal belongings and architecture.

Can a 3D scan help us preserve the world as we know it? Will archaeology in the future take place through virtual 3D collections? Can we move our lives into virtual space with all our beloved material things?

The technology is used to create a backup of the scanned object or person. It is a movement from the physical to the virtual world. A change of state. A phase transition.
