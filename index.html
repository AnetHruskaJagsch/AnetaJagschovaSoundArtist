Aneta Jagschová

To capture little everyday absurdities, to hold on, to remember. To fight the memory. To understand. That is why I create.

I always tend to sympathise with the weak and the overlooked, trying to bring attention to it – spaces, meanings, acts, rituals, people. Even my main medium is like that – sound as the forgotten undercurrent in all processes. From soundscapes of everydayness to film sound. I draw inspiration from endless wandering around the globe. Always on the hunt for substance, for presence, for awareness, for laughter. Acting in the roles of Artist, researcher, and teacher which makes for a restless way of making meaning. In between. In the raw.

My main urge is to archive, to protect, to hold on to trophies and collections, and an even bigger one is to laugh at myself and the whole Anthropocene for having this urge.

I always oscillate between preserving and intervening, finding and contextualising. Between public and private space. And what is my ultimate goal? Just to let go.
